There's a lot that happens in the creative process. The exploratory phase, production, self-doubt, making logos bigger, more self-doubt, and final delivery.  
  
But those aren't always hardest part. Sometimes, it's what happens after.  
  
It's been a full week since I was in Miami, in a room full of creatives, sharing what I do after weeks of prepping. And after I got home, when I finally sat down at my computer to keep doing what I do best... there was nothing there.  
  
This wasn't burnout, and it wasn't a lack of ideas. I had more energy and more ideas than I'd had all year, thanks to the week prior.  
  
But nothing had the WOW! factor. Everything just felt flat.  
  
I once heard that creative block is really just the fear of making something bad. And after riding the high of a big project, starting over with something new and unproven can produce that exact fear.  
  
So I figured: let it be bad.  
  
Tons of scribbles in notebooks. Canvases in Fresco named "Untitled" that will never get a name, because I deleted them after an hour. Crumpled-up scrap paper. Ideas thrown into a a text file or chat box for brainstorming. Endless amounts of bad work.  
  
And now it's Friday. I'm sitting here with a pile of bad ideas and a few of them look like they might be something. More versioning, more iteration. Who knows? Maybe they get deleted too. But for now, I'm making bad work.  
  
And I'm enjoying it.
