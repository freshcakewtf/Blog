## The quick version

It started with a Zine about daisies. Then it turned into three posters about Ethernet cables in my basement, because apparently that's where inspiration lives now.

- **Fresco** — sketch and vector linework
- **Firefly Boards** — turn the illustrations into fully rendered images
- **Express** — layout, text, export, done

---

## What worked

Express clicked way harder than I expected. No artboard clutter, no file-naming pressure, just design. I even brought Firefly's AI assistant in to brainstorm poster copy — and immediately caught it fumbling a networking fact, which was its own funny little lesson in double-checking your collaborators, human or not.
