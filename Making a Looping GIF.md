### Back to my roots

New video up. I made a looping GIF using three apps I actually use every day: **Adobe Fresco**, **Firefly Boards**, and **Adobe Express**.

For those who don't know, I spent years as "the Giphy guy" — 2.8 billion views and counting. So this one felt like going home.

## The quick version

- **Fresco** — rough sketch in non-photo blue, clean linework with the basic round vector brush, fill, details. Old Bristol board habits die hard.
- **Firefly Boards** — illustration in as a reference, prompt out a final character render, generate some turnarounds, then drop the character into a scene. Same image for first and last frame = clean loop.
- **Express** — import the video, drop it on the timeline, add meme text, export as a GIF.

The character turned out to be a self-portrait. Muck boots, shorts, mud, leftover snow. Just me in my 30s.

---

## Why this workflow

Human-made, AI-assisted. I draw the character. The tools help me scale it into something loop-able and shareable without losing the hand-drawn DNA.

GIFs work best when they carry a single emotion. Keep the prompt simple. Let the loop do the talking.
